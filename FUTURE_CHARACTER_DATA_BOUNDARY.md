# Future character data boundary

The author has deferred designing the full behavioral records. The present story graph and composer must work without them. Source-backed cues, dialogue and lexical mentions are available as reviewable references; they are not a complete personality or a proven account of motives.

The related local [node hierarchy research](<D:/0/Noir Fiction Engine/Architecture/Node_System_Contracts_v1/01_HIERARCHY_AND_ROLE_RULES.md>) describes a Character SuperNode as an actor-root network, with distinct persona/form identities and optional behavior, memory, cognition and other child networks. The [port and evidence contract](<D:/0/Noir Fiction Engine/Architecture/Node_System_Contracts_v1/02_NODE_PORT_AND_EVIDENCE_CONTRACT.md>) discusses typed ports, pinned records, provenance and distinct source/interpretation/approval states. These are design references from a related project, not evidence that Narrative Composer implements that larger system or that a full Super JSON schema exists here.

This candidate currently accepts pinned local `narrative.story-index.v1`, `narrative.dialectic.v1`, and `narrative.behavior.v1` packages in their implemented domains. The actor or each form has an independently addressable port supporting inherit, extend, override and replace. Package ownership, version/schema and content hash are checked. The installed legacy profiles are pending review, and the synthetic rule fixtures used for tests are not authored character records.

When the author supplies the actual Super JSON/Deep Character record and format, implement and validate an explicit versioned adapter against that schema, including ownership, provenance, migration, review status, content hashes and dependency invalidation. Preserve unknown fields and source evidence. Do not guess the record, silently convert unsupported data, or treat a connected package as approved canon. Until then, the port is a connection boundary, not a claim that the future record is implemented.
