## COLD OPEN

### `INT. LING-EIDOLESSA HOUSE – STELLA'S BEDROOM – NIGHT (FIVE MINUTES EARLIER)`

Storm light flashes outside the bay window. Inside, the room is a velvet-draped VIP lounge masquerading as a suburban bedroom. Crushed maroon pillows, stacked Taschen art monographs, the low electronic hum of an eighteen-inch ring light, and three women from Boobers who moved in during spring break and simply never left.

ZOOEY, forty, reclines along the velvet chaise, drawing an emery board across square acrylics with lazy, practiced sweeps. KRISTEN folds a scrap of black silk mesh with surgical precision. DANUTA taps her phone screen, illuminated by the cold blue glare of social feeds. On the carpet near the carved footboard, TOSHI and SNOT sit cross-legged before an old CRT television, losing another round of vintage fighting games.

A DELIVERY WOMAN in her thirties stands frozen in the doorway, cardboard parcel clutched against her uniform, staring at STELLA as if her central nervous system just unplugged.

Stella, 21, clad in a fitted canary-yellow polo, khaki shorts, and clear non-prescription frames worn purely for ironic hipster cachet, scrawls her signature across the digital pad without looking up. The delivery woman's fingers remain locked to the clipboard.

**DELIVERY WOMAN:** "Sorry. Can I just say—you're—"

**STELLA:** *(breezy, practiced)* "Every single day, babe. The parcel?"

The delivery woman lets go, backs out through the threshold, walks shoulder-first into the hallway doorframe, murmurs a stunned apology to the drywall, and stumbles down the stairs.

**ZOOEY:** *(without looking up from her nail)* "You broke another straight girl."

**STELLA:** "I didn't do anything."

**KRISTEN:** "You breathed."

**STELLA:** "That's the family siren thing. Mom Rainee's side. It's not personal."

**DANUTA:** "It's a little personal."

Stella smirks into the vanity mirror. Scrawled across the glass in crimson lipstick: *LISA SILVER — THURSDAY, 8. DON'T BE COOL.* She flicks an empty paper twist into the porcelain tray. A faint lemon-powder fragrance drifts into the air.

**ZOOEY:** "Did you dump the whole packet?"

**STELLA:** "Just the limited-edition Sephora shimmer highlighter. If Hayley's going to march in from that strip-mall cardio cult carrying Mom Rainee's entire ancestral backside and acting like a fitness messiah, she can sparkle while she scratches. I even left a comment on her new workout blog noting that her content looks like fresh old protein powder—all chalk, zero nutrition."

**KRISTEN:** "You're vicious. Come here."

Stella executes a practiced circuit—kissing Zooey's cheek, Kristen's collarbone, Danuta's forehead—just as a muffled, glass-shattering SHRIEK rips upward through the floorboards from the living room below.

**STELLA:** *(delighted)* "And there goes the palette. Showtime. Nobody touch my girlfriends while I'm downstairs, Snot."

**SNOT:** *(reverent, from the carpet)* "We wouldn't dare, Stella! Mostly because Zooey threatened to staple my ears to the drywall."

Stella floats into the hallway, hips swaying with effortless swagger. Toshi and Snot watch the doorway.

**TOSHI:** *(in Japanese; subtitled)* *"[She walks into a hurricane of glute-fueled vengeance carrying a paper umbrella.]"*

**SNOT:** "Totally, dude. She's the coolest person alive."

## ACT ONE — CHUNK 1: The Wound & The Dumbbell

### `INT. LING-EIDOLESSA HOUSE – LIVING ROOM – NIGHT`

Storm light strobes through the blinds. Inside, the atmosphere glows pink-gold, heavy with the sharp reek of ozone, warm coconut oil, and scorched lavender.

Hayley's neon yoga mat, tubular resistance bands, and chrome weight rack occupy the east half of the room; Stella's velvet couch, smothered in photography folios, dominates the west. Centered between them upon an antique mahogany side table perches Francine's pink ceramic flamingo lamp, Gloria, unbothered, delicate, and profoundly doomed.

HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS stands center carpet, digging short nails into her forearms. Her trademark green headband is gone, replaced by a voluminous, glossy black blowout, plumped lips, and a neon tie-dye sports bra paired with matching tie-dye dolphin shorts that have lost all control of her colossal, shelf-like new rear—two massive, planetary hemispheres exerting their own localized gravity. White static sparks crackle across her skin, popping and dying against the rug fibers.

HOLLY GRAHAM, blonde and sharp in a plum HG jumpsuit and heavy gold medallion, lounges in the armchair with a clipboard and an oily mason jar—the former 'Queen of Dance Cardio' who witnessed Hayley's lobby awakening and instantly demoted herself to personal assistant.

STELLA sprawls across the velvet couch cushions, laughing hard enough to snort. Pale blue fox-fire flickers momentarily in the iris of her left eye.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(scratching furiously)* "It's crawling under my skin, Holly! Like your HG body-sorceress zap and Roger's stupid nanobots woke up my fox genes all at once and they want OUT!"

**HOLLY GRAHAM:** "Breathe through the tingle, boss. At HG, I thought I was the Queen of Dance Cardio—until I zapped you at the front desk and your bloodline snapped the mahogany counter in two. Take a pull of the HG oil smoothie. Flax, chia, CBD, coconut, and just a splash of Pennzoil. Don't smoke a cigarette for thirty-six hours or you'll level the block."

**STELLA:** "Oh my GOD. Look at the blowout and the tie-dye dolphin shorts! You went down to the strip mall looking for an undercover investigative scoop, and five minutes later you're a suburban thirst-trap! What happened to 'unmasking the corporate machine'?!"

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** "This isn't funny! My Sephora palette is ruined, and I know it was you! The itching powder was a nice touch, by the way. Real mature."

**STELLA:** "I have no idea what you're talking about." *(smug)* "But if I did, I'd say the powder was a public service."

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(twirling a glossy black curl, horrified by her own nasally vocal fry)* "And why do I sound like this?! 'It is suuuch bullcrap that my skin is itching!' Seriously, I went down there to dismantle the capitalist commodification of women's glutes, and now I'm, like, a huge businesswoman who pops and locks! My tie-dye sports bra is 100% fair-trade bamboo, but honestly, this peach is too massive for systemic oppression!"

**HOLLY GRAHAM:** "Don't fight the vocal fry, Mommy. You're a mogul now. We've already got forty women in Distressed Muscle Tanks outside waiting to hand Lindsay their bank routing numbers."

**STELLA:** *(sitting upright, locking sights on Holly)* "Wait—hold on. Did the blonde cult leader just call my sister 'Mommy'? Let's start with YOU, 'Holly Graham.' Or should I spell it how it sounds—Hologram?"

Holly's commercial smile freezes into a flat line.

**STELLA (CONT'D):** "Please. You run an overpriced strip-mall cardio cult for desperate housewives who think neon spandex counts as spiritual awakening! You're a glorified infomercial with ankle weights, and now you’re lurking in our living room calling my sister 'Mommy'? Did the lease run out on your studio, or are you just scouting new suburban marks to buy your Distressed Muscle Tanks?!"

**HOLLY GRAHAM:** *(eyes narrowing)* "Careful, little girl. At the studio, we call that energy a Commitment Vampire. And you know what we tell a CV to do."

**STELLA:** "Oh, I'm terrified. Look at your 'masterpiece'!"

Stella turns from Holly and jabs a sharp finger toward Hayley's straining tie-dye shorts.

**STELLA (CONT'D):** "Hayley went in to 'smash the fitness patriarchy,' and she came back looking like an over-inflated parade float! Look at that thing—it doesn't even obey basic gravity! What did she do, hook you up to an industrial tire pump just so you could pretend you have a fitness empire?! You don't have a brand, Hayley—you have a walking zoning violation!"

Hayley freezes. The fingernails stop dead against her ribs. She snaps around, hips driving a furious arc—and the massive, shelf-like tie-dye shelf swings wide, catching Francine's framed 'Langley Falls Yard of the Month' plaque square across the brass hook. The frame launches off the wall and SMASHES against the baseboard in a shower of glass.

**STELLA (CONT'D):** "See?! Your butt just assassinated Mom's plaque! It's a sentient wrecking ball!"

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(eerily calm, smoothing her dolphin shorts)* "I just haven't mastered spatial awareness with hips this wide yet. If Other Mom can walk through a doorway without taking out the framing, so can I."

Hayley's eyes widen as the actual insult lands. Her pupils contract into amber slits.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS (CONT'D):** "Wait—a walking zoning violation?!"

She slams a palm onto her hip, thrusting the tie-dye curve out with fierce, unshakeable pride.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS (CONT'D):** "It is NOT plastic, and it is NOT an over-inflated parade float! Look at this—I have a waist and, like, three more waistlines! My silhouette literally has its own gravitational field! Holly only gave me the starter spark—the rest is two decades of Mom Rainee's fox genetics finally waking up! Once Roger's nanobots finish locking in, this peach isn't even at half capacity. You just called the family crown jewel a zoning violation!"

Holly sets down her clipboard and circles Hayley's hips like an art dealer assessing a marble statue.

**HOLLY GRAHAM:** "Nobody with working retinas would call that an accident, Stella. I've morphed a thousand women at HG, and none of them blew the doors off my lobby. When I hit Hayley with 'Transform,' her Rainee bloodline hijacked my spell, gave her two stuck-together onions of pure divinity, and made me say 'Yes, Mommy.' That's why I work for her now."

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(chin lifted, heavy vocal fry)* "It's NOT a BBL, Holly. Seriously, I am, like, 100% organic birthright, okay? I can't even Google myself right now because too many hits would come up."

**HOLLY GRAHAM:** "Nobody said BBL, boss."

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** "…Good."

Hayley turns her gaze back to Stella. The pride hardens into predatory focus.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS (CONT'D):** "You think this is funny."

**STELLA:** "I think it's hilarious! You walked into a dance-cardio cult reeking of weed at two P.M. on a Wednesday, got zapped by a hologram, and now you're standing there in tie-dye booty shorts with a rash, waiting for applause. Wait—aren't you married to Jeff? Or did the HG oil smoothie finally nuke your taste in men, too?"

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(flaring)* "Shut it! Jeff and I are, like, going through a thing! It's complicated!"

**STELLA:** *(mocking baby voice)* "Aww, my big sister's a fitness mogul now! That's so cute! You're nearly as powerful as me. Nearly."

Hayley's scratching stops entirely. She palms her tie-dye hip as a deep pulse of pink-gold fox-fire rolls up from her glutes through her spine. She reaches down, snatching a ten-pound cast-iron dumbbell off the chrome rack. Her fingers sink into the solid metal. She rolls the iron in her grip like warm taffy; the bell creases, groans, and snaps cleanly in two. Both halves thud into the carpet pile.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(blinking at her palm, then smirking)* "…Huh. Guess I AM gym people now."

Holly scoops up the tray of oil smoothies and drags her armchair six feet deeper into the shadows.

**HOLLY GRAHAM:** "Smoothie's safe on the coaster, boss. Pace yourself—freshly unlocked sorceress batteries burn hot."

The living room ceiling lamps flicker, dimming as the air thickens with cold copper and ozone.

## ACT ONE — CHUNK 2: Spatial Friction & Armchair Slapstick

### `INT. LING-EIDOLESSA HOUSE – LIVING ROOM – CONTINUOUS`

Hayley navigates toward the mahogany coffee table, buoyant hips swinging sideways. The draft from her mass rustles the stacked Taschen art monographs. Her left hip clips the edge of the antique armchair, sending the entire heavy oak frame sliding three inches across the hardwood with a screech of scraped varnish, narrowly missing the scatter of photography folios and the side table where Francine's pink ceramic flamingo lamp Gloria perches.

**STELLA:** *(flinching, pulling her bare knees up on the velvet couch)* "Hayley, watch the floorboards! You just moved an eighty-pound armchair with your hip! If you sit down, you're going to punch a crater straight through to the furnace!"

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(settling into the armchair, cushions groaning under the colossal displacement)* "This bourgeois furniture was engineered for colonized, sedentary frames. My mass requires a wider ideological footprint."

**STELLA:** *(flailing an arm toward the scraped wood)* "Your 'ideological footprint' just ruined the hardwood! You don't have an ideology, Hayley—you have an oversized ego in dolphin shorts and an obsession with looking brand-worthy!"

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(leaning back, deadpan vocal fry thick as motor oil)* "And yet, here I am, Stella. Living rent-free in your room, your head, and your furniture. While you sit there with your shirt ripped open like a Victorian chimney sweep."

**STELLA:** *(tugging the frayed polo seams over her ribs)* "At least my clothes obey the laws of physics! You're a walking, floating contradiction! Eco-righteousness on the outside, pure unhinged thirst-trap on the inside!"

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(smirking, tapping her mason jar)* "The future is now, sister. And it's forty percent wider than you."

## ACT ONE — CHUNK 3: The Collision of Delusions

### `INT. LING-EIDOLESSA HOUSE – LIVING ROOM – MOMENTS LATER`

The swinging kitchen door kicks open with a BANG.

FRANCINE storms into the room, smelling of warm coconut oil, scorched lavender, and sharp deli mustard. In her left hand she carries a steaming glass bowl of yellow potato salad; in her right, an oversized wooden mixing spoon. Her floral apron is dusted with paprika.

Her eyes scan the living room, completely bypassing Hayley's colossal, room-dwarfing silhouette as if it were a potted ficus, locking instantly onto Stella's canary-yellow polo.

**FRANCINE LING:** *(rushing to the velvet couch, wooden spoon poised)* "Oh, sweetheart! Look at you! Your collar is completely ruined! Those poor little exposed collarbones—Momma's got warm potato salad! Open wide, my sweet little boy!"

**STELLA:** *(swatting the wooden spoon away in sheer panic)* "Francine, STOP! Get that spoon away from my face! I'm twenty-one years old, my name is STELLA, and I am NOT a boy!"

**FRANCINE LING:** *(undeterred, trying to spoon-feed Stella with maternal aggression)* "Shh, shh, don't be stubborn, Stevie! You've always been cranky when your clothes get torn during roughhousing! Let Momma feed you the good carbs!"

Hayley drifts upright out of the armchair, her massive rear swaying with buoyant inertia, white ozone sparks crackling across her tie-dye waistband. She stares at Francine in complete, indignant disbelief.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(raising her mason jar, voice cracking through her vocal fry)* "Excuse me?! Mom! Hello?! Two hundred pounds of unassisted, fair-trade biological perfection floating right in front of the bay window, and you're trying to burp a grown woman on the sofa?!"

Francine waves a dismissive hand toward Hayley without breaking eye contact with Stella's collar.

**FRANCINE LING:** *(distractedly, to Hayley)* "That's nice, dear, take your lawn clippings into the kitchen—Mommy is nursing her sweet boy back to health."

Hayley's jaw drops. Her high-status composure instantly shatters into frantic, unhinged sibling jealousy.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(slamming her mason jar onto the side table)* "A SWEET BOY?! Are you blind?! I have a waist and three extra planetary circumferences, and you're doting on a flat-chested poser who ruined my Sephora palette?!"

**FRANCINE LING:** *(whipping around, wooden spoon dripping mustard-yellow potato salad)* "Hayley, do not raise your voice at your brother! He's had a very traumatic afternoon!"

**STELLA:** *(scrambling backward over the couch armrest)* "I AM NOT HER BROTHER!"

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(throwing her hands wide, laughing with bitter theatricality)* "And the Oscar for Best Supporting Delusion goes to... Francine Smith!"

Stella bolts for the hallway door, squeezing past Hayley's immense floating silhouette as Francine charges after her with the bowl of potato salad, shouting across the trembling living room.

## ACT ONE — CHUNK 4: The Hallway Bottleneck & Structural Containment

### `INT. LING-EIDOLESSA HOUSE – UPSTAIRS HALLWAY – CONTINUOUS`

Stella scrambles around the mahogany banister, bare feet slapping the hardwood, both hands clutching the seams of her canary-yellow polo over her ribs. Behind her, the living room door rattles on its hinges.

HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS glides into the narrow hallway archway. Her immense, buoyant hips wedge squarely between the white radiator and the doorframe pine, groaning under two hundred pounds of displaced mass. She bobs two inches above the runner rug, balancing her mason jar of green spirulina sludge with serene, low-gravity stability.

**STELLA:** *(skidding to a halt, pressing back against the wallpaper)* "Hayley, move! You're wedged into the archway like an oversized parade float! Francine is right behind me with a mixing spoon!"

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(sipping calmly through her reusable bamboo straw, deadpan vocal fry humming)* "I am not 'wedged,' Stella. I am establishing an architectural sanctuary. This hallway was constructed under patriarchal residential zoning codes that refuse to accommodate liberated female mass. You're just projecting your suburban claustrophobia onto my sovereign silhouette."

**STELLA:** *(pointing down the hall)* "I'm not projecting claustrophobia, you're physically blocking the fire escape route! Your hips have their own postal code!"

FRANCINE charges into the hallway, apron fluttering, wooden mixing spoon dripping yellow potato salad onto the floorboards. Her eyes lock directly onto Stella's collar.

**FRANCINE LING:** *(reaching past Hayley's floating hip with maternal intensity)* "Stevie! Hold still! Momma's trying to do up your little polo buttons! Where did all your buttons go?! Did the boys at the comic shop tear them off?!"

**STELLA:** *(ducking under the wooden spoon, clutching her chest)* "THEY'RE MY SHIRT BUTTONS, NOT STEVIE'S! Francine, open your eyes! Hayley is floating three inches off the floor with an ass wider than the radiator, and you're diagnosing me with missing buttons?!"

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(turning with a dismissive wave, white ozone sparks crackling across her tie-dye waistband)* "Mom, please. Do not equate my ancestral fox-spirit expansion with her cheap schoolyard trauma. Roger's nanobots are conducting a masterclass in localized bio-gravity, and frankly, you're disrespecting my brand."

**FRANCINE LING:** *(waving the potato salad spoon dismissively toward Hayley)* "Not now, Hayley, Mommy is helping your brother through a delicate growth spurt. Stevie, eat the carbs!"

## ACT ONE — CHUNK 5: The Ambush & The Shattered Flamingo

### `INT. LING-EIDOLESSA HOUSE – UPSTAIRS HALLWAY / LIVING ROOM – CONTINUOUS`

Stella presses her back flat against the wall, trapped between Hayley's massive floating hip and Francine's advancing wooden spoon. The warm aroma of deli mustard and potato starch fills the tight corridor.

Stella looks up at Hayley's unbothered expression, calculating furiously.

**STELLA:** *(lowering her voice, urgent staccato)* "Hayley. Listen to me. If she shoves that spoon into my mouth, she's moving on to you next. She's going to make you drink whole milk to 'settle your stomach.'"

Hayley freezes mid-sip. Her glossy black brows knit together.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(vocal fry cracking with sudden vulnerability)* "Whole milk? Industrialized dairy pus? Over my decentralized, plant-based dead body."

**STELLA:** *(nodding rapidly toward the kitchen)* "Then we need a diversion. Where did Roger leave the nanobot calibration remote?!"

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(leaning down, whisper humming with static)* "Downstairs freezer. Behind the Totino's Pizza Rolls. Wrapped in aluminum foil next to the synthetic estrogen."

Stella pivots toward Francine with instant theatrical panic.

**STELLA:** *(pointing down the stairs)* "Mom! Stevie put all the junk food in the freezer! Toaster pastries, pizza rolls, high-fructose corn syrup—it's ruining the whole household glycemic index!"

Francine stops dead, wooden spoon trembling in mid-air. Her eyes go wide with domestic terror.

**FRANCINE LING:** *(gasps, clutching her bowl to her chest)* "Not my sweet boy's glycemic index! Who let refined sugar into this house?!"

Francine wheels around and sprints back toward the kitchen, fuzzy slippers squeaking against the hardwood, shouting warnings to the refrigerator.

Silence settles over the corridor. White static sparks fizzle out along the baseboard.

Stella slides down against the baseboard, exhaling a long, shuddering breath, her hands resting on her knees.

**STELLA:** *(looking up at Hayley, smirk returning)* "Phew. Sibling armistice achieved. Now move that two-ton wagon so I can get back to my couch."

Hayley drifts down, blocking the doorway completely. Her eyes narrow into amber slits, locking onto Stella with cold, predatory intent.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** "Armistice? You think this is an armistice?"

A crackle of pink-gold lightning arcs between Hayley's knuckles.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS (CONT'D):** "You called my blog 'fresh old protein powder.' You spiked my Sephora palette with itching powder. You called my divine birthright a walking zoning violation. And Mom is running around trying to spoon-feed you like her precious little brother Stevie?"

She raises her palms. Pink-gold energy ERUPTS from her core, roaring through the hallway to envelop Stella in a lattice of shimmering light.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS (CONT'D):** "Holly taught me the front-desk transformation—and my fox genes supercharged it. So in the words of HG: you're a Commitment Vampire, Stella, and you can suck my butt! You want Mom to treat you like a boy?! I'm gonna strip away that fake poser-nerd act of yours and morph you into what you really are—a REAL boy nerd in a can!"

**HOLLY GRAHAM:** *(calling from the living room armchair)* "Give her the front-desk special, Mommy! Hit her with Transform!"

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** "TRANSFORM!"

Stella scrambles to flee toward the living room, but the pink energy net lassoes her mid-stride!

Stella's blue fox shield flares around her—a thin, shivering bubble.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(almost gently)* "You never trained, did you? You just liked how the shield looked in mirror selfies."

**STELLA:** "I—shut up!"

The pink energy bites into the blue barrier like lye. The shield CRACKS, dissolving into dying motes of blue static.

**STELLA (CONT'D):** "WHAT ARE YOU—?! My shields should be holding! HOW ARE YOU—"

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** "—Breaking through? Way more power than you now, sis. Like, three more waistlines of power? Seriously, I am like a huge magical businesswoman right now, and it's honestly not even fair?"

**STELLA:** "There it is! The voice! You sound like an unhinged influencer who just got denied entry to a VIP lounge!"

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(teeth grinding)* "It's called vocal resonance, you little poser! Keep laughing!"

The glowing net YANKS Stella airborne, slinging her violently back into the living room!

**STELLA:** "Whoa! Hey! Not the lamp! NOT THE LAMP!"

Her sneaker snags the coffee table edge, launching a half-full soda can into the air. The net whips her backward into the antique mahogany side table. Gloria the pink ceramic flamingo teeters on one skinny leg for a breathless beat—and SHATTERS against the hardwood floor in a violent spray of pink porcelain shards!

Dead silence falls. Hayley's posture drops; for an instant she looks eleven years old, staring at a broken family heirloom.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS:** *(small)* "…Gloria."

Then her eyes find Stella coughing on the rug, and her expression resets. She thrusts her tie-dye curve wide, deliberate and hard.

**HAYLEY FITNESS GODDESS (CONT'D):** "Oops. Mom's gonna kill us. Actually? Totally worth it."

**STELLA:** "That was Mom's favorite lamp, you psycho!"
