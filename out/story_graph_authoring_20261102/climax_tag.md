## EPISODE TAG: Nobody Learns Anything

### `INT. LING-EIDOLESSA HOUSE — LIVING ROOM — THE NEXT MORNING`

Sunlight streams through the bay windows, casting warm amber rectangles across the hardwood floor. 

Francine Ling lounges across the velvet chaise, wearing a high-cut crimson gym unitard that strains against a sculpted Jennifer Lopez special rear. 

Beside her, Influencer BBL Hayley reclines on the main sofa, her planetary tie-dye dolphin shorts occupying two full cushions with low-gravity ease. On the floor rug, Big Tiddy Steve sits surrounded by stacked Taschen art monographs and a half-eaten plate of crustless peanut-butter-and-jelly sandwiches cut into neat triangles.

INFLUENCER BBL HAYLEY
(smoothing her tie-dye waistband, deadpan vocal fry humming)
"I learned my lesson, Mom. Never fall for fitness cults again, and never get high on weed on a Wednesday. But honestly, if you gave me proper guidance instead of your benevolent neglect strategy, I would never have joined Holly's cult in the first place."

FRANCINE LING
(taking a bite of a triangle sandwich, unbothered)
"Yeah, I know. I am sorry, Hayley. But as a mother, you can't be there every second. Eventually, you let your children down. So how do you prepare them? By letting them down early, and often—"

Before Francine can finish her speech, Hayley's smartphone BUZZES loudly on the coffee table. 

The screen illuminates with caller ID: **HOLLY GRAHAM (QUEEN OF CARDIO)**.

Hayley presses the speakerphone button.

HOLLY (O.S.)
(through speaker, smoky and seductive)
"I wanted to apologize for the jungle trials, sweetie. But I knew you had the inner strength to power through for all of us. I've made fantastic progress readying the product for commercial market. We are past the jungle beta and moving into what my legal team calls a 'regulator-free offshore acceleration window.' Ninety days of outcomes, testimonials, before-and-after footage, and absolutely no one in a federal windbreaker asking why our injectable contains proprietary Vanderbooben compound. After that, we begin influencer seeding, regional studio rollout, and a direct-to-consumer campaign where a happy woman jogs through a farmer's market while pretending she always looked like that. Given that you were instrumental, I want to grant you a percentage of the gross profits and invite you back as my number one. You can manage the regional brand. Also, I want to reward you with private time—personal time with the Queen of Cardio, if you catch my drift."

Hayley's pupils flare wide. She bites her lower lip, trembling from head to toe with helpless, starry-eyed devotion.

FRANCINE LING
(reaching for the phone)
"Wait a minute! Put down that phone, Hayley! She is too powerful for you! Resist the cult!"

INFLUENCER BBL HAYLEY
(voice dropping into a ragged, breathless whimper)
"I am trying, Mom... It is really, really hard..."

HOLLY (O.S.)
"Come home to Mommy, Hayley."

INFLUENCER BBL HAYLEY
(screaming at the ceiling in complete ecstasy)
"YES, MOMMY! SIR! OH YES, MOMMY!"

Hayley scrambles off the couch, her massive 200-pound rear bouncing with low-gravity momentum, and bolts for the front foyer.

FRANCINE LING
"Wait, Hayley! Didn't you just say you learned your lesson about cults?!"

INFLUENCER BBL HAYLEY
(babbling in a frantic, stammering rush as she yanks the deadbolt)
"I have to do, um... bumping stuff with beautiful girls and butts and creamy tender thighs and, um, yeah... something fun! Bye now!"

The front door swings wide. 

Big Tiddy Steve scrambles upright from the floor rug, pushing thick black glasses up the bridge of his/her nose with a single trembling index finger, oversized canary-yellow polo fluttering.

BIG TIDDY STEVE
(voice cracking into a high-pitched nerdy squeak)
"What in the name of street fashion is happening right now?! Why does everyone in this house have their own localized gravitational pull?! Why is Hayley running to central Africa to bump butts?!"

Roger struts out of the dining room wearing his red satin HG Inner Circle bomber jacket with gold zipper pulls, wielding a five-foot brass curtain rod like an Olympic javelin.

ROGER
"Okay, no! Back, I say!"

Roger repeatedly pokes Big Tiddy Steve squarely in the stomach with the brass curtain rod.

BIG TIDDY STEVE
(hopping backward across the rug, arms flailing)
"Ouch! Ouch! Stop poking me with the curtain rod! I have delicate nerd ribs now! Ouch!"

ROGER
"Back upstairs with your comic books and rocket-ship underwear! Begone, nerd! This is an elite peach household today! No nerds allowed!"

Big Tiddy Steve scrambles up the staircase on all fours, slamming the bedroom door shut.

FRANCINE LING
"Thank you, Roger. Now... Hayley! What about your husband, Jeff?! Isn't running off with your aerobics coach technically infidelity?!"

INFLUENCER BBL HAYLEY
(pausing at the front porch railing, frowning blankly)
"Je...ff? What? Who? What is a Jeff?!"

FRANCINE LING
"Oh, never mind. She can barely form words. Have fun, dear, clapping that onion booty."

Hayley sprints down the driveway, her cheeks clapping together like thunderclaps in the quiet suburban morning.

Francine sighs, smoothing her crimson leotard over her newly expanded, glazed-ham hips.

FRANCINE LING (CONT'D)
"Well, I failed miserably as a mother saving my daughter from the glute cult. I think she is a lesbian now, and her marriage is over. Did we learn anything from this entire adventure, Roger? I think I lost the moral."

ROGER
(admiring his gold zipper pulls)
"I don't think so, Frannie. You got a celebrity rear, we discovered this honeydew melon is delicious, and I confirmed that my extraterrestrial glutes are a planetary treasure. Kapow! Kapow! Kapow!"

Roger drops into three rapid, dramatic squats, his grey hips flexing with athletic precision.

FRANCINE LING
"Well, that's good enough for me. Time for exercise."

Francine stands beside Roger in front of the bay window. Together, they drop into rhythmic, synchronized squats.

ROGER / FRANCINE LING
"Activate your core! Left cheek! Right cheek!"

Fast, energetic dance-cardio fitness music swells.
