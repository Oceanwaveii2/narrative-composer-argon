## SCENE 1: The Breakup, The Booth & The Starch Can

### `INT. THE BUTTON NIGHTCLUB — 11:47 P.M.`

Cigarette smoke and Drakkar Noir hang thick over a crowd dressed like they raided Don Johnson's closet. The DJ spins Depeche Mode loud enough to rattle the mirrored ceiling tiles.

The booths sit elevated on a carpeted platform—three steps up, velvet rope unhooked and dangling. From here you can see the whole bar. The whole crowd. Everything.

Near the bar, a drag queen in eight-inch lucite heels pours Fireball straight into a bachelorette's mouth; the liquid arc catches a spotlight like molten cinnamon glass.

FRANCINE is wedged into the deepest curve of a horseshoe booth—the vinyl more patch than seat. Mascara streaks glisten. Her cocktail dress was prettier before the crying. A pale ring-indent ghosts the finger where a diamond used to live. The booth squeaks when she shifts; the sound sticks like gum.

Across the table: KELLY, balanced on her knees so she can lean in. Her martini glass sweats; the napkin under it has bled into pulp. Lipstick prints climb the stem like evidence.

KELLY
"Stan was... okay, you know what Stan was? Stan was a timeshare. The brochure looked amazing. Ocean view, balcony, the whole—"

She waves her hand, sloshing her drink.

KELLY (CONT'D)
"—whatever. Point is, you show up, and it's a parking lot next to a Denny's."

FRANCINE
(half-laugh, half-sob)
"I liked the brochure."

KELLY
"Everybody likes the brochure. The brochure's a liar. Stan was a participation trophy with a pulse. That's it. That's the whole man."

Francine flinches.

KELLY (CONT'D)
"No, stop. You need to hear this. You think he's out there 'test-driving' new models? Francine, he couldn't find your clitoris with a flashlight and a campus map. Guy thinks foreplay is quoting Scarface and asking if you 'wanna play with his little friend.'"

A drunk frat guy stumbles past, overhears, and does a spit-take. Kelly doesn't blink.

KELLY (CONT'D)
"He dumped you—Ling, the girl who taught her TAs how to curve grades for charity—because his frat brothers convinced him he could pull a Theta. Newsflash: Stan Smith irons his jeans. Not washes. Irons. Creases down the front like he's attending a job interview at IBM... in 1957. And he uses starch. I've seen the can, Francine. It's next to his loofah."

FRANCINE
(looped)
"...He has a loofah?"

KELLY
"Had. You think that's bad? He bragged about his 'portfolio.' It's forty bucks in Bitcoin and a savings bond from his nana. He took you to Olive Garden for your six-month anniversary and asked if you could 'go Dutch on the breadsticks.' The man uses cologne that comes in a plastic bottle shaped like a tomahawk. He smells like a Snapchat filter."

Francine's laugh turns into a honk.

KELLY (CONT'D)
"He dumped you via GroupMe. Wrote 'think we should c other ppl' at 2 a.m., then immediately posted a meme of a crying Michael Jordan. You know why? Because he's already fucking his roommate's ex. Yeah. Brenda. The one who thinks The Handmaid's Tale is a love story. She's gonna find out real fast that Stan's idea of eating out is the dollar menu and a prayer."

Kelly slams her glass down. The stem cracks.

KELLY (CONT'D)
"You're out here crying like you lost a Pulitzer. He lost a Ling. The man's about one spiral notebook away from moving back into his mom's craft room, where she still has his participation ribbons from youth soccer. He wasn't a boyfriend, Fran. He was a placeholder. A human pop-up ad. And you? You're the girl who's gonna make partner while he's still making excuses. So sober up. We've got a bottle of Stoli with his name on it, and I'm not talking about drinking it."

She gestures to the dance floor, where bodies slam together like a trash compactor.

KELLY (CONT'D)
"Now get up. We're gonna find you a man who knows what a Roth IRA is and doesn't call his mother 'Mommy-o.'"

Francine stares at the broken glass. Then at Kelly. Then at the ghost-ring.

FRANCINE
"...Did you really see the starch can?"

KELLY
"Lemon-scented, Fran. The man's a serial killer."

Francine stands. Wobbles. Then smiles.

KELLY (CONT'D)
"Now stop spiraling. I didn't put on Spanx to watch you cry into a watered-down vodka."

Francine sighs, gives in. Her gaze drifts across the crowd—shoulder to shoulder, pulsing like one drunken organism.

Then her eyes snag on something. She blinks. Blinks again.

FRANCINE
(slowly)
"Kel."

KELLY
"Mm?"

FRANCINE
"Does that woman have... is that a tail?"

Kelly follows Francine's eyeline to the bar.

## SCENE 2: The Fox on Two Barstools

### `INT. THE BUTTON NIGHTCLUB — BAR — CONTINUOUS`

RAINEE.

She occupies two barstools like they were built for her alone. A red so saturated it looks wet spills from scalp to tail-tip—every strand electric, scattering neon like fiber-optic cable. The fox ears aren't costume-shop fleece; the inner folds are peach velvet, twitching to the beat. Her tail—broad as a canoe paddle—sways in hypnotic figure-eights, flower clips (tiny enamel daisies, kiwi-green) glinting like runway lights. The sequined mini-dress strains at the seams, rhinestones winking SOS each time she shifts.

FRANCINE
(finally really looking)
"...Holy shit."

KELLY
"I mean, that ass is DISGUSTING."

Beat.

KELLY (CONT'D)
(laughing)
"I want it."

Francine's breathing goes shallow. Her hands flutter to her lap, fingers twisting the hem of her dress.

FRANCINE
(voice small)
"She's looking at me. Kel—she's looking right at me."

KELLY
"She's STARING. At YOU. Like you're the last slice of pizza at 2 AM."

FRANCINE
"I can't just—she's not even—what even IS she?"

KELLY
"Who cares? She wants you. Go. Experiment. Fuck Stan."

Francine starts to stand. The booth makes a wet peeling sound—her thighs unsticking from vinyl. She wobbles on borrowed confidence.

KELLY (CONT'D)
"Shoulders back. If you panic, just compliment her tail. People with tails probably like that."

FRANCINE
"That's your advice?"

KELLY
"I'm making this up as I go. Now move."

Francine steps off the platform, into the crowd. A shoulder catches her. Someone's drink sloshes. She mumbles "sorry, sorry" as she squeezes between bodies—loses sight of the fox-woman—finds her again—loses her—

Back at the booth, Kelly climbs onto the bench, arms overhead.

KELLY (CONT'D)
(shouting after her)
"Oh hell yes, Francine! Show that fox who's boss!"

Several heads turn. Francine's shoulders hunch. She keeps walking.

## SCENE 3: The Napkin & The Six Redhead Shapeshifts

### `INT. THE BUTTON NIGHTCLUB — BAR — CONTINUOUS`

She's three steps from the bar when she realizes Rainee isn't on those barstools anymore.

A tail slides around her waist from behind. Warm fur. Firm muscle underneath.

RAINEE
(already there, mouth near her ear)
"Hey there, gorgeous. Mind if I wipe my seat off?"

Francine spins. Rainee is RIGHT THERE—didn't see her move, didn't hear her approach.

Without waiting for an answer, Rainee plucks a napkin from the bar. Folds it. Strokes it along Francine's cheekbone—gentle, slow. Two passes. A third under the eye. A soft pat at the corner of her mouth.

The club noise drops to a muffled heartbeat.

FRANCINE
(processing)
"...Did you just call my face your—"

She giggles despite herself.

FRANCINE (CONT'D)
"That's not a chair."

RAINEE
(still wiping, serene)
"Shh. Gotta prep the leather."

She finishes. Pats Francine's cheek once.

RAINEE (CONT'D)
"And I'm very picky about where I sit. This feels like quality."

FRANCINE

"Oh my God. That's so filthy."

Rainee's thumb brushes the place where the ring used to be—barely a touch, but Francine feels it in her spine.

RAINEE

RAINEE (CONT'D)

The air around Rainee starts to shimmer. Francine blinks—thinks it's the vodka—but no. The woman in front of her is rippling.

The club keeps going. Music thumps. Someone laughs too loud. At the bar, a guy works through a plate of nachos like it's his job, cheese stringing from chip to mouth.

Nobody sees this. Just Francine.

RAINEE (CONT'D)
"What's your name?"

FRANCINE
"Francine. Franny. Either."

RAINEE
"Franny."

Her tail tightens around Francine's waist.

RAINEE (CONT'D)
"Don't freak out."

FRANCINE
"Wh—"

Rainee shimmers.

## SCENE 4: Reinflation & Furniture Loyalty

### `INT. THE BUTTON NIGHTCLUB — BAR — CONTINUOUS`

The shimmer folds inward. All six collapse—Madonna, Christie, Heather, Kim, Debbie, Bo—shuffling into Rainee like cards into a deck.

Fox ears flick back into place. Red fur catches the light. Tail uncurls from wherever it went.

And then—

Her hips start to spread.

It's not fast. It's like watching bread rise in time-lapse. Her thighs thicken. Her ass swells—slowly, inevitably—reclaiming territory it had temporarily surrendered to celebrity accuracy.

Francine can hear it—a soft creak, like leather settling into shape. The sequins on Rainee's dress shift, rearranging around new geography.

The barstool GROANS.

A second barstool slides over—on its own, nobody pushing it—and nestles against the first. They CLICK together, widening, adjusting. The seats seem to expand, reshape, until they're less "two barstools" and more—

FRANCINE
(staring)
"Did those chairs just..."

Rainee settles back into them. Comfortable. Regal. Like a queen taking her throne.

RAINEE
"They know their place."

FRANCINE
"Your ass has FURNITURE LOYALTY?"

RAINEE
"Everywhere I go. Chairs just... know."

FRANCINE
"That's a THRONE. That's two barstools that turned into a THRONE."

RAINEE
(delighted)
"Furniture loves me. It's a whole thing."

Francine's eyes are locked on the newly restored real estate. She's tilting her head like she's trying to solve a math problem.

FRANCINE
"Where did it GO though? During the—when you were Christie, you were just... normal-sized. Where was all THAT hiding?"

RAINEE
"Trade secret."

FRANCINE
"It's like watching a balloon animal but in reverse. But also not a balloon. But also—"

She stops. Her hand is reaching out. She yanks it back.

FRANCINE (CONT'D)
"Sorry. I wasn't going to—"

RAINEE
(amused)
"You want to touch it."

FRANCINE
(too fast)
"No. Maybe. It's just—scientifically—I need to know if it's REAL. For science."

RAINEE
"For science."

FRANCINE
"It could be a hologram! An illusion! A very convincing... cushion situation."

Rainee laughs. Shifts on her throne. The chairs creak happily.

RAINEE
"Go ahead. Give it a squeeze."

Francine's hand hovers. Retreats. Lands.

Her eyes go wide.

FRANCINE
"Oh my God."

RAINEE
(grinning now)
"Mm-hm."

FRANCINE
"It's so SOFT. But also... firm? How is it both?"

RAINEE
"Alien physiology."

FRANCINE
(squeezing again)
"It's like memory foam but ALIVE."

RAINEE
(laughing)
"I'm gonna put that on a business card."

FRANCINE
"I could fall asleep on this."

RAINEE
"Buy me dinner first."

Francine yanks her hand back. Face crimson.

FRANCINE
"Sorry—I didn't mean—that was weird, I made it weird—"

RAINEE
(tail curling with amusement)
"You made it perfect."

She pats the throne-chairs beside her. They somehow widen another inch.

RAINEE (CONT'D)
"Sit with me."

Francine looks at the chair. At Rainee. At her own cheese-covered hands.

FRANCINE
"I have nacho on me."

RAINEE
"I've survived worse."

Francine sits. The chair adjusts to accommodate her—just slightly—like it's being polite.

FRANCINE
(under her breath)
"Did the chair just HUG me?"

RAINEE
"Told you. Furniture loyalty. It's contagious."

STRANGER
"What the FUCK is wrong with you people?"

He storms off. Neither woman notices.

Francine grips the bar with both hands. One has cheese on it. The other is still warm where it was kissed. She touches her cheek without thinking—cheese transfers to her face. She doesn't notice.

Long beat. Then:

FRANCINE
"Why were they all redheads?"

RAINEE
"That's your first question?"

FRANCINE
"Christie Brinkley. Blonde. Bo Derek. Blonde. Madonna changes every five minutes. But you—"

She gestures at Rainee's hair.

FRANCINE (CONT'D)
"Red. Every single one."

Rainee twirls a strand of her hair around one finger. Casual.

RAINEE
"Red's my lucky color. Always has been."

FRANCINE
"Lucky how?"

RAINEE
(counting on fingers)
"Fortune. Prosperity. Good deals. My grandmother swore by it—said any Eidolessa who changed their red would lose their way home."

She shrugs, grinning.

RAINEE (CONT'D)
"I don't actually believe that part. But I do keep closing contracts, so..."

She flips her hair over one shoulder—performative, playful.

RAINEE (CONT'D)

(processing)

RAINEE

She gestures at herself. The tail does a little flourish.

"There it is."

"You okay, Franny?"

(looking at cheese on her hands, then at Rainee, then at the throne-chairs, then back at the cheese)

Some GUY at the next table looks over.

GUY

(still looking at Francine, serene)
"You were saying?"

FRANCINE
(eyes wide, pointing at the fallen guy)

RAINEE
"He was being rude. Your laugh is adorable."

FRANCINE
"I think I'm in love with you."

RAINEE
(grinning)

RAINEE (CONT'D)
"There's a back room with fewer nachos and a door that locks. Interested?"

"YES."

Rainee's tail tightens around Francine's waist, guiding her through the crowd. Francine's hands still haven't left Rainee's hips.
