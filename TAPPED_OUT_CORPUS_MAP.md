The percentages and Steve node are the user's requested graph distinctions, not an assertion that every outline documents each intermediate step. The outline's Edna-like and princess appearances are candidate form records until reviewed. A state label, costume or display name is not a new actor by itself. The same actor and form identities may be referenced in multiple editions while observations, knowledge, causes, memories and accepted histories remain scoped to the selected edition. `Steve` is a contextual alias for Stella in this material; do not globally equate every character named Steve with Stella.

For the active Steve state, preserve all three author-specified facts together: **Stella's actor identity and memories continue; her body becomes her own version of Steve; and her persona and behavior become Steve-like.** The character may act differently from everyday Stella because of this transformation; the system must not erase that change by forcing her baseline personality onto the state. It also must not assume that she has another person's memories or that every other form necessarily changes personality. Track body, persona, memory, agency and Francine's influence as related but individually inspectable facets, without reducing the transformation to a cosmetic overlay. The form's data port can later hold an explicitly authored behavioral dataset; none has been supplied yet.

The corresponding paths for Rainee, Hayley/Haley and Francine must likewise preserve one actor and individually addressable forms. The prior request names Rainee's separate Episode 0 celebrity forms and Peter form; Hayley's base, Roger and BBL forms; and Francine's base, nurse and J.Lo forms. Distinct forms do not imply distinct personalities. Each actor and form needs a data connection that can inherit shared data or accept an explicitly chosen override. Deep Character/Super JSON datasets have not been supplied; no record or reaction should be invented to fill the empty ports. An externally imposed persona in this outline is source-supported story state, not proof that all transformations modify personality.
